<details class="bird-gear" markdown="block">
<summary>🎒 My Gear</summary>

- **Nikon COOLPIX P950**: Unmatched reach. Awful contrast-based focus, it crawls after 3 burst shots, and suffers if lighting isn't ideal.
- **Nikon DF-M1 Dot Sight**: At extreme zoom, locking onto warblers or tracking birds in flight is challenging through the viewfinder. The hot-shoe dot sight solves this with an unmagnified reticle to instantly acquire targets. Due to its height above the lens, vertical parallax means near vs. far subjects require slight elevation adjustments, but horizontal tracking stays dead-on.
- **Pixel 9 Pro**: Sometimes strapped to Vortex Diamondback HD 8x42 binoculars.
- **Nikon Z50II**: Occasionally borrowed 💃.
